Manchester is the city where I’m doing the CodeYourFuture Intro to Programming course. 
The classes are in the city centre, so it’s easy to walk around and explore before or after the sessions.
There’s a really nice café near the venue called Nur Café. It’s a small, cosy place with friendly people 
and tasty Mediterranean food. It feels comfortable and welcoming, like a spot where you can chill,
eat something good, and relax before class.
A fun fact about Manchester is that it was the first industrialised city in the world. 
It played a big part in the Industrial Revolution, and today it’s still known for its creativity, culture, 
and amazing music scene.
